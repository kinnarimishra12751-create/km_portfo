01About

Kinnari has spent over twelve years moving between the classroom, the research lab and — more recently — the university's ICT Cell, where she now helps faculty across departments put new digital tools to work.

Her research sits at the intersection of machine learning and everyday problems: recognising faces from limited datasets, reading sentiment out of customer reviews, and finding the signal inside large, messy datasets. She is currently a PhD scholar in AI at Parul University while continuing to teach, publish and guide student projects.

As Academic Coordinator and Manager of the ICT Cell, she now spends much of her time on a different kind of mentorship — helping colleagues across Parul University's institutes adopt AI tools, LaTeX and the university's exam and classroom modules in their own teaching and research.

02Impact

Empowering educators with AI

What began as an initiative to introduce AI tools has grown into a wider shift in how educators approach their work — reaching faculty across multiple institutes at Parul University.

Watching colleagues actively explore, adopt and integrate AI into their academic practice has been genuinely rewarding — and it reinforces a simple belief: when educators evolve, the entire learning ecosystem transforms. As a computer science educator and PhD scholar in AI, the aim is to keep bridging the gap between traditional education and AI-driven innovation, one session at a time.

Atal Faculty Development Programme

ATAL FDPs are faculty-development programmes run under AICTE's Training and Learning Academy to sharpen domain knowledge, pedagogy and research skills among faculty at AICTE-approved institutions. Programmes run as Basic or Advanced FDPs, usually blending online theory with offline, hands-on practical sessions.
